Our method

From idea to installation

01

Feasibility

Every piece is assessed before any commitment. We tell you early on what is feasible, at what cost and within what timeframe.

02

Technical design

Our design office turns the drawing into shop drawings: sections, joints, what will hold up in daily use and not just on paper.

03

Prototype

We are in the workshop to sign off colours, finishes and real-world comfort, and to have corrections made before launch.

04

Production

Scaling up to series production is where things typically drift. We lock down the process so the hundredth room matches the first.

05

Inspection & delivery

Inspection happens in the workshop, not on site. Our Luxembourg team then handles customs and transport all the way to the site.
