Hôtel Verdaine

A Parisian hotel, from the superior room to the junior suite. Full-height lacquered panelling, headboards, wardrobes and minibars built into the joinery, curved banquettes and coffee tables — and a signature room in burl marquetry. From one room category to the next, the freestanding furniture picks up the woods and fabrics of the fixed joinery: the hotel reads as a single whole.
